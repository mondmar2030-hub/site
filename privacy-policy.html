Privacy policy — Al Amane Location

Updated: 6 November 2026
Android application: ma.alamane.al_amane — version 1.3.0

1. Operator and contact

This application is presented by AGENCE AL AMANE DE LOCATIONS DE VOITURES, LOCAL COMMERCIAL N 20 RUE BIR ANZARANE, Goulmima (M), Morocco (MA).
For questions about this policy or app data, contact support@almane.com. You may also write to the agency at the postal address above.

2. Scope of this version

This version is an offline car-rental planner with a sample catalogue. Vehicle categories, prices and extras are examples. Car illustrations are AI-generated and bundled with the app. No AI service is called while you use it.
Drafts are not sent to the agency. The app does not confirm reservations or process payments.

3. Information processed on your device

When you enter them, the app uses your name and phone number for your local profile and drafts. These fields are required to save a draft, but are not required to browse the catalogue.
The app also stores favourites, selected vehicle, pickup and return dates, selected extras, estimated price, draft reference and local cancellation status.
It stores which items you mark complete on the pickup and return checklist. The checklist is a personal reminder, not verification by the agency.
Vehicle comparisons and budget calculator inputs (distance, fuel consumption, fuel price and other costs) are processed on the current screen and are not saved by the app.
The car price calculator also processes your selected category, rental dates, daily rate and optional extras on the current screen without saving them or sending a request to the agency.
Search text and filters control the catalogue display. The app does not save a search history.
Profile information and drafts are stored locally. We do not automatically receive them: this version has no reservation server, remote account or synchronisation between devices.

4. Purposes and transmission

Information is used for the functions you request: displaying favourites, restoring your profile, drafts and checklist, comparing sample vehicles, estimating trip costs and producing a summary.
The Android app has no advertising, analytics, remote crash reporting or payment integration. It does not transmit profile or draft information to a server or sell that information.
It does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or identity documents. It does not read SMS messages or include advertising tracking.
Flutter and preference-storage libraries provide the interface and local storage; these uses do not send your draft contents to the agency.

5. Clipboard and actions you choose

When you select Copy, your name, phone number and draft details are placed on the device clipboard. The app does not read the clipboard to look for other content.
The operating system and apps into which you paste this text may process it under their own policies. Review the summary before sharing it. Copying does not automatically send anything to the agency.
If you voluntarily email support, your message and the information you include are communicated outside the app to handle your request. Do not send payment cards, driving licences or identity documents by email.

6. Retention and deletion

Profiles, favourites, drafts and checklist progress remain locally until you delete them or clear app data. This version has no automatic deletion period.
To delete them, open My space, select Erase my local data and confirm Erase. This removes the profile, favourites, drafts and checklist stored by the app on this device. If storage fails, the app displays an error and you should try again. If the error continues, use your device settings to clear app storage.
You can delete a single draft in Requests using Delete draft and confirming Delete. You can remove individual favourites and reset checklist progress separately.
You can also use your device settings to clear app data. Uninstallation and any restoration mechanisms depend on your device operating system.
Cancelling a draft does not delete it: it remains in local history with a cancelled status. Use Delete draft or local data erasure to remove it.
Erasing app data does not remove text already copied to the clipboard, emails or copies shared with other apps or recipients. Manage those copies separately.
This version has no remote account to close or reservation database that we can erase remotely.

7. Data protection

Preference storage is not encrypted by the app itself. Protection depends on application isolation, device locking and operating-system protections. Protect access to your device and do not use this app to store sensitive documents.
Android automatic app backup is disabled in the manifest. Device-specific backup or transfer behaviour may vary. We do not operate a remote backup service for this app.
We cannot guarantee absolute security or retrieve local data after deletion or device loss.

8. Your control over information

You can edit your profile in My space and delete local information as described above. For questions about your information or applicable rights, contact support@almane.com.
Because drafts remain on your device, the agency cannot view, correct or delete them remotely in this version.

9. Audience and external services

The app is intended for planning car rentals and is not designed for children. Confirm actual rental age, licence, insurance and rental requirements directly with the agency.
This policy covers the Android app described above. Google Play, Android, your email provider and the website hosting this policy may process their own technical data under their own policies. We do not claim those independent services process no data.
The web preview uses browser storage and depends on its hosting provider. If published as a web service, it requires information specific to that deployment.

10. Changes to the application

This policy must be updated if online reservations, authentication, payments, notifications, analytics or other data processing are added. An updated policy will be available in the app and on the public privacy page. The date above identifies this version.

11. This privacy webpage

The supplied privacy webpage contains no forms, advertising or analytics scripts. Its own display controls run in your browser and do not set cookies or use browser storage.
Opening a hosted webpage sends technical requests to its hosting and network providers, which can include your IP address, browser information and requested page. Those providers may process or retain technical records according to their own policies and the host operator's settings. This is separate from local Android app storage. The hosting operator should check its provider's practices before publishing the page.

YOUR LOCAL CONTROLS

Ready to clear your saved plans?

  1. Open My space in the Android app.
  2. Choose Erase my local data.
  3. Confirm Erase.

This clears the profile, favourites, drafts and checklist on this device. Clipboard text and copies shared elsewhere need to be managed separately.
